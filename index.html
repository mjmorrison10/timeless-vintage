Vintage 101

Knowledge is power — especially when you're hunting for authentic pieces. Here's what every vintage shopper should know.

Terminology

Vintage vs. Antique vs. Retro

Antique = 100+ years old. Vintage = 20–99 years old with collectible appeal. Retro = Modern reproduction of a vintage style. We sell authentic vintage and antiques — no reproductions.

Authentication

Spotting the Real Deal

Check zipper type (metal = older), seam construction (serged = newer), label materials, and fabric content tags. When in doubt, ask us — we authenticate every piece we sell.

Care

Caring for Vintage

Hand wash or dry clean. Store away from sunlight. Use padded hangers. Never store in plastic. Treat vintage like what it is — something worth preserving.

Investment

Vintage as Investment

Quality vintage appreciates. A Chanel bag bought for $200 twenty years ago is worth $2,000+ today. Buy what you love, wear it well, and it may reward you later.

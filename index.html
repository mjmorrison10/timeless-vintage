About Timeless Vintage

Where History Meets
Modern Style

Located on historic Glassell Street in the heart of Orange, Timeless Vintage is more than a store. It is a destination for people who appreciate the craftsmanship, character, and stories behind vintage pieces. We believe the best things in life have a past.

Our team spends countless hours sourcing, authenticating, and curating each item in the shop. We are not interested in filling shelves with average finds. We want every piece to excite you the way it excited us when we discovered it.

Whether you are a seasoned collector or just starting to explore vintage, our knowledgeable staff is here to help. We love talking about the history behind our pieces almost as much as we love finding them.
